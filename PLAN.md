Read **all six** documents in `./plan/` in numerical order before designing schematics, laying out PCBs, writing firmware, or generating 3D CAD:

2. **[`plan/01_POWER_PROTECTION_AND_USB_ISOLATION.md`](plan/01_POWER_PROTECTION_AND_USB_ISOLATION.md)**

3. **[`plan/02_AUDIO_CHAIN_CV_DACS_AND_MIDI_SCHEMATIC.md`](plan/02_AUDIO_CHAIN_CV_DACS_AND_MIDI_SCHEMATIC.md)**
   - Pin-level schematics for `4× Neutrik NMJ4HCD2` (`IN L` passively normalled to `IN R` via `TN`; `OUT L/R` `TN` strictly isolated), **Boutique Popless/Clickless Stereo Relay True-Bypass** (`2× Omron G6K-2F-Y-TR DC5` relays + `2× Toshiba TLP172AM` PhotoMOS Opto-FET optical shunt mutes + `2.2 MΩ` DC bleed network + soft-slewed coil drive + `250 ms` POR/brownout thump blanking), `1 MΩ` `TL072CDT` stereo guitar preamp (`+18.1 dB` at `12 o'clock` `IN TRIM`) & `NE5532DR` return stage (`-18.1 dB` at `12 o'clock` `OUT TRIM` = `0.0 dB` unity & `0°` phase), `+3.3V`-clamped `MCP6002` guitar peak/pluck detector, `8×` `-8V..+8V` `MCP4822` + `TL072` CV/1V-Oct/Gate channels, `C&K JS202011AQN` right-angle DPDT `MIDI TYPE A / B` switch, `H11L1` (`DIP-6`) optocoupler, and `74HC14D` pure hardware `MIDI THRU`.
